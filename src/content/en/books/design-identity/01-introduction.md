The idea behind WBasic's full name is **Tungsten BASIC**. It sets a direction for the design: strong without being loud, precise enough for practical work, and open to what comes next. The visual identity neither recreates a retro green terminal nor dresses itself so formally that beginners hesitate to touch it.

## The language and its appearance should agree

The language uses words such as `Procedure`, `Let`, and `Structure` to make meaning visible. Its visual system follows the same principle: headings reveal hierarchy, links look like destinations, code preserves every character, and important states have written explanations rather than relying on color alone.

**WBasic** is the language's name; **Wbasic.** is the wordmark. Its quieter shape ends with a small teal dot as a recognizable signature. The dot does not also need to work as a button, a traffic light, and an exclamation mark.

## An identity that leaves room for the content

Mist backgrounds provide reading space, Charcoal text provides strong contrast, and Teal, Sage, and Sand appear for a reason. Shapes have gently rounded corners, with spacing based on 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, and 48 pixels. The aim is a reference page that remains comfortable over time: recognizable at a glance, then unobtrusive while the reader untangles a difficult error.

Identity is more than decoration added at the end. A small set of shared rules helps different people make consistent decisions—and should spare everyone a few meetings about whether a green is green enough.
