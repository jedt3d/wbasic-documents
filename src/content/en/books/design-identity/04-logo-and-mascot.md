WBasic has three marks with distinct roles: the **element emblem** connects to Tungsten BASIC, **Wbasic.** shows the language's full name, and the **wombat face** adds an approachable presence to books and tools. All three share the same palette.

## The element emblem and language name

The element emblem combines a rounded square, the number **74**, and **W**, referring to tungsten. Its Teal dot is the same signature as the period in **Wbasic.** When the emblem appears where readers may not know the language, keep the Wbasic. name nearby so they need not guess whether the subject is chemistry or programming.

**The bottom of the dot must align with the baseline of the W**, in both large emblems and small icons. Do not let the dot hang below it. The Wbasic. wordmark uses IBM Plex Sans and always retains its final dot.

## Two versions of the front-facing mascot

Use the front-facing wombat head, either **unframed** or **inside a rounded square**. Both eyes use Teal `#237F83`, matching the logo's dot. The nose, mouth, and facial lines use Charcoal to preserve clear detail.

The website header places the **unframed mascot first, followed by Wbasic.** The framed version suits icon areas that need a defined boundary; it does not replace the unframed head in the header.

## Consistent use

- Preserve the original proportions. Do not stretch, squash, rotate, or add shadows to the logos.
- Leave clear space around a mark at least equal to the Teal dot's diameter.
- Use SVG for websites and work that must scale; use transparent PNG when a ready-to-place image is needed.
- Choose the asset for its destination background, and check that the number 74 and the eyes remain legible at the final size.
- If the element emblem is too small for 74 to remain readable, enlarge it or use the Wbasic. wordmark instead.

These editable vector assets follow the selected visual direction. When a new hand-drawn version is introduced, update both the master assets and the files used by the website together.
