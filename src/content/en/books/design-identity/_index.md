title: "WBasic Design and Identity"
description: "The ideas, colors, typography, logos, and mascot that give WBasic and its documentation a shared character"
weight: 4
---

This small book explains why WBasic looks the way it does. It starts with the character we want to convey, rather than a list of CSS values, then introduces the five colors, typography for Thai, English, and code, and the shared logos and mascot.

Its four chapters should fit into a coffee break. Return to them when designing a new page: they offer a common direction without turning every page into the same poster.
