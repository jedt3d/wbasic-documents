## WBasic project

Identify a Suspect from Clues. The project needs to keep track of **suspects, their traits, and clues**.

## Proposed method

Filter out suspects who do not match each clue until one answer remains.

## Project-specific acceptance check

A red-hat clue eliminates suspects without a red hat. This is an acceptance example to test when the source is written; it has not passed yet.

## Remaining gap

Verify the clue data structure and seeded case generation.
