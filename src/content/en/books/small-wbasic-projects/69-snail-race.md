## WBasic project

Race Slowly Moving Snails. The project needs to keep track of **the snails, their positions, and the finish line**.

## Proposed method

Choose each snail's progress from a fixed seed on every tick.

## Project-specific acceptance check

No snail moves beyond the finish line. This is an acceptance example to test when the source is written; it has not passed yet.

## Remaining gap

Verify the speed PRNG, TUI timer, and multicell drawing.
