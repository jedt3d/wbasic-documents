# Project 44 Plan: Navigate a Two-Dimensional Maze

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Navigate a Two-Dimensional Maze.
Data: a maze map, the player, and the exit.
Method: Check for a wall before moving in response to a key.
Acceptance check: A move into a wall leaves the player in place.
Remaining gap: A fixed maze is feasible; verify TUI keys and viewport behavior.

Credit: Al Sweigart, author of The Big Book of Small Python Projects. This WBasic chapter plan reworks the idea in new words.
