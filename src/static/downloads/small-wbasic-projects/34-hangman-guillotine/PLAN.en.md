# Project 34 Plan: Guess Letters Before the Drawing Is Complete

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Guess Letters Before the Drawing Is Complete.
Data: the target word, guessed letters, and penalty count.
Method: Reveal every position containing a correct letter and avoid charging for repeated guesses.
Acceptance check: Guessing A in BANANA reveals three positions.
Remaining gap: Choose words from a word bank and verify Unicode-scalar handling and multiline drawing.

Credit: Al Sweigart, author of The Big Book of Small Python Projects. This WBasic chapter plan reworks the idea in new words.
