# Project 08 Plan: Print a Monthly Calendar

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Print a Monthly Calendar.
Data: the year, month, and weekday on which the month begins.
Method: Compute leap years and arrange dates in seven columns.
Acceptance check: February 2000 has 29 days; February 1900 has 28.
Remaining gap: Implement and test weekday and leap-year arithmetic in the project; there is no DateTime or Calendar API.
