# Project 17 Plan: Practice Addition with Dice

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Practice Addition with Dice.
Data: two die values, their addition problem, and the user's answer.
Method: Generate the die values from a fixed seed and check their sum against the answer.
Acceptance check: For 3 + 4, accept 7 and reject 6.
Remaining gap: Random problems need a tested seeded PRNG.

Credit: Al Sweigart, author of The Big Book of Small Python Projects. This WBasic chapter plan reworks the idea in new words.
