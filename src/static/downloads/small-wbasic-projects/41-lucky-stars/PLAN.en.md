# Project 41 Plan: Bet on a Selected Star

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Bet on a Selected Star.
Data: the selected star, stake, and result.
Method: Choose a star from a fixed seed and apply the scoring table.
Acceptance check: The remaining balance never becomes negative.
Remaining gap: Specify the payout rules and verify the PRNG.
