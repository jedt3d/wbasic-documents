# Project 63 Plan: Play the Royal Game of Ur

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Play the Royal Game of Ur.
Data: piece paths, die results, and safe spaces.
Method: Roll from a fixed seed and move pieces according to the rules for each space.
Acceptance check: A roll of zero does not move a piece.
Remaining gap: Specify the dice rules and verify the PRNG and command input.

Credit: Al Sweigart, author of The Big Book of Small Python Projects. This WBasic chapter plan reworks the idea in new words.
