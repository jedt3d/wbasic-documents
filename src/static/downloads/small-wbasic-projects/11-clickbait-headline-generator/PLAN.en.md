# Project 11 Plan: Generate Headlines from Templates

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Generate Headlines from Templates.
Data: three word categories and headline templates.
Method: Choose words with a fixed seed and insert them into a template.
Acceptance check: The same seed produces the same headline.
Remaining gap: Verify the seeded PRNG used to select templates and words.

Credit: Al Sweigart, author of The Big Book of Small Python Projects. This WBasic chapter plan reworks the idea in new words.
