# Project 39 Plan: Turn an Ant and Flip Grid Cells

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Turn an Ant and Flip Grid Cells.
Data: the ant's position and direction, and cell colors.
Method: Read the current cell color, turn the ant, flip the color, then move one cell.
Acceptance check: On a white cell, the ant turns right and leaves the cell black.
Remaining gap: Array logic is feasible; continuous play needs verification of the TUI timer.

Credit: Al Sweigart, author of The Big Book of Small Python Projects. This WBasic chapter plan reworks the idea in new words.

Original idea: https://inventwithpython.com/bigbookpython/project39.html
