# Project 27 Plan: Animate Fish in a Text Tank

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Animate Fish in a Text Tank.
Data: each fish, its position, and swimming direction.
Method: Move fish and reverse them at an edge while preserving the tank border.
Acceptance check: A fish at the right edge turns left.
Remaining gap: Verify the TUI timer, viewport, and PRNG controlling movement.

Credit: Al Sweigart, author of The Big Book of Small Python Projects. This WBasic chapter plan reworks the idea in new words.
