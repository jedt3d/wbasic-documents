WBasic Design and Identity

The ideas, colors, typography, logos, and mascot that give WBasic and its documentation a shared character

This small book explains why WBasic looks the way it does. It starts with the character we want to convey, rather than a list of CSS values, then introduces the five colors, typography for Thai, English, and code, and the shared logos and mascot.

Its four chapters should fit into a coffee break. Return to them when designing a new page: they offer a common direction without turning every page into the same poster.

01 Introduction: The Character of WBasic From Tungsten BASIC to a calm, precise, approachable language with room to grow 02 The Language's Colors Five identity colors, their roles, and how to use them without sacrificing readability 03 The Language's Typography IBM Plex for prose, Thai, and code, with a practical reading hierarchy 04 The Language's Logos and Mascot The W element emblem, Wbasic. wordmark, and wombat face, with light and dark examples and downloadable assets
