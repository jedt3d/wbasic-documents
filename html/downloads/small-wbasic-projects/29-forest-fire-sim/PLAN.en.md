# Project 29 Plan: Simulate Fire Spreading on a Grid

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Simulate Fire Spreading on a Grid.
Data: a grid of trees, fire, and ash.
Method: Compute the next generation from the old grid and seeded spread decisions.
Acceptance check: A cell burning in this generation becomes ash in the next.
Remaining gap: Verify the PRNG, TUI timer, and grid-edge conditions.
