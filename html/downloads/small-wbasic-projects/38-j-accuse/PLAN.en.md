# Project 38 Plan: Identify a Suspect from Clues

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Identify a Suspect from Clues.
Data: suspects, their traits, and clues.
Method: Filter out suspects who do not match each clue until one answer remains.
Acceptance check: A red-hat clue eliminates suspects without a red hat.
Remaining gap: Verify the clue data structure and seeded case generation.
