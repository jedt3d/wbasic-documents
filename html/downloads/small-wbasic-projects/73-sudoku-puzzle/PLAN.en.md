# Project 73 Plan: Validate or Solve a Sudoku Grid

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Validate or Solve a Sudoku Grid.
Data: a 9×9 grid and candidates for empty cells.
Method: Check rows, columns, and boxes, then solve by backtracking.
Acceptance check: A row containing two 5s is invalid.
Remaining gap: A validator is feasible; a puzzle generator must verify unique solutions and its PRNG.

Credit: Al Sweigart, author of The Big Book of Small Python Projects. This WBasic chapter plan reworks the idea in new words.
