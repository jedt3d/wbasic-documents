# Project 37 Plan: Escape Robots Pursuing the Player

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Escape Robots Pursuing the Player.
Data: the player, robots, and walls.
Method: Move the player first, then move robots to reduce distance and check collisions.
Acceptance check: Walking into a wall leaves the player's coordinates unchanged.
Remaining gap: Verify TUI events and timer behavior, along with grid-collision rules.
