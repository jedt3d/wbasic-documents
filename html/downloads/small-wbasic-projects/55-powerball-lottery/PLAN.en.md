# Project 55 Plan: Simulate Lottery Tickets and Draws

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Simulate Lottery Tickets and Draws.
Data: tickets, winning numbers, and simulation rounds.
Method: Generate nonrepeating numbers from a fixed seed and count matches.
Acceptance check: No ticket contains a duplicate number.
Remaining gap: Verify the PRNG, uniqueness of numbers, and limits of large simulations.

Credit: Al Sweigart, author of The Big Book of Small Python Projects. This WBasic chapter plan reworks the idea in new words.
