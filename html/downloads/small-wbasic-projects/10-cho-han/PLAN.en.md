# Project 10 Plan: Bet on Odd or Even with Two Dice

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Bet on Odd or Even with Two Dice.
Data: two die results and an odd-or-even guess.
Method: Generate two values from 1 to 6 and compare the sum's parity with the guess.
Acceptance check: A roll of 2 and 5 has an odd sum.
Remaining gap: Verify a seedable dice PRNG; WBasic has no public random API.

Credit: Al Sweigart, author of The Big Book of Small Python Projects. This WBasic chapter plan reworks the idea in new words.
