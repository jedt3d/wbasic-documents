# Project 02 Plan: Simulate Shared Birthdays in a Group

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Simulate Shared Birthdays in a Group.
Data: the group size, birthdays numbered 1–365, and trial count.
Method: Generate birthdays from a fixed seed and count trials with at least one shared birthday.
Acceptance check: A group of one person has zero birthday collisions.
Remaining gap: Define and verify a seeded PRNG for simulated birthdays; WBasic has no public random API.

Credit: Al Sweigart, author of The Big Book of Small Python Projects. This WBasic chapter plan reworks the idea in new words.

Original idea: https://inventwithpython.com/bigbookpython/project2.html
