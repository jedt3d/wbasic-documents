# Project 23 Plan: Draw on a Grid with Direction Keys

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Draw on a Grid with Direction Keys.
Data: the marked grid and pen position.
Method: Read direction keys, move the pen within bounds, and mark visited cells.
Acceptance check: On a 3×3 grid, moving right then down leaves marks in three cells.
Remaining gap: Verify TUI key events and preservation of the drawing grid through resizing.

Credit: Al Sweigart, author of The Big Book of Small Python Projects. This WBasic chapter plan reworks the idea in new words.

Original idea: https://inventwithpython.com/bigbookpython/project23.html
