# Project 09 Plan: Play Carrot in a Box

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Play Carrot in a Box.
Data: two boxes, the carrot's position, and each player's answer.
Method: Choose the carrot's box from a fixed seed and hide it from the guesser until a box is chosen.
Acceptance check: The guesser sees the boxes but not the carrot before the reveal.
Remaining gap: Use a verified seeded PRNG to choose the box, and test whether the console presentation actually hides information from the guesser.

Credit: Al Sweigart, author of The Big Book of Small Python Projects. This WBasic chapter plan reworks the idea in new words.
