# Project 69 Plan: Race Slowly Moving Snails

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Race Slowly Moving Snails.
Data: the snails, their positions, and the finish line.
Method: Choose each snail's progress from a fixed seed on every tick.
Acceptance check: No snail moves beyond the finish line.
Remaining gap: Verify the speed PRNG, TUI timer, and multicell drawing.

Credit: Al Sweigart, author of The Big Book of Small Python Projects. This WBasic chapter plan reworks the idea in new words.
