# Project 46 Plan: Count Frequencies in a Million Dice Rolls

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Count Frequencies in a Million Dice Rolls.
Data: frequency buckets for sums from 2 to 12.
Method: Roll two seeded dice one million times and count each sum in its bucket.
Acceptance check: The counts across all buckets total one million.
Remaining gap: Verify the PRNG, Integer range, and runtime for one million iterations.

Credit: Al Sweigart, author of The Big Book of Small Python Projects. This WBasic chapter plan reworks the idea in new words.
