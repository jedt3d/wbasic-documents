# Project 75 Plan: Track a Target Card Through Swaps

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Track a Target Card Through Swaps.
Data: three card positions, the target, and the swaps.
Method: Choose seeded swaps and update the target position after every swap.
Acceptance check: Starting at position 1, swaps 1–3 then 2–3 leave the target at position 2.
Remaining gap: Verify the shuffling PRNG and, if swaps are animated, the TUI timer.

Credit: Al Sweigart, author of The Big Book of Small Python Projects. This WBasic chapter plan reworks the idea in new words.
