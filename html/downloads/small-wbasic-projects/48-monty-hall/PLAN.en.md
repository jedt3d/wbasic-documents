# Project 48 Plan: Compare Staying and Switching in Monty Hall

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Compare Staying and Switching in Monty Hall.
Data: three doors, the prize door, and the host's reveal.
Method: Reveal an unchosen losing door, then compare the stay and switch outcomes.
Acceptance check: If the prize is behind door 1 and door 2 is chosen, revealing door 3 makes switching a win.
Remaining gap: Verify the PRNG and the host rule that only a losing door is opened.

Credit: Al Sweigart, author of The Big Book of Small Python Projects. This WBasic chapter plan reworks the idea in new words.
