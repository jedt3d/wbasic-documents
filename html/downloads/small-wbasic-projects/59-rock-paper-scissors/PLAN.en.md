# Project 59 Plan: Play Rock, Paper, Scissors Against the Computer

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Play Rock, Paper, Scissors Against the Computer.
Data: the player's and computer's moves.
Method: Choose the computer's move from a fixed seed and compare it against the winning pairs.
Acceptance check: Rock beats scissors and loses to paper.
Remaining gap: Verify the PRNG used for the computer's choice.

Credit: Al Sweigart, author of The Big Book of Small Python Projects. This WBasic chapter plan reworks the idea in new words.
