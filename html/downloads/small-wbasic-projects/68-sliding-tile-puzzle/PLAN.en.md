# Project 68 Plan: Slide Numbered Tiles into the Empty Space

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Slide Numbered Tiles into the Empty Space.
Data: the board, empty space, and move direction.
Method: Shuffle a solved board through legal moves so the resulting puzzle remains solvable.
Acceptance check: A tile adjacent to the empty space can move; a distant tile cannot.
Remaining gap: Verify generation of solvable boards and the shuffling PRNG. Text-command input is feasible.

Credit: Al Sweigart, author of The Big Book of Small Python Projects. This WBasic chapter plan reworks the idea in new words.

Original idea: https://inventwithpython.com/bigbookpython/project68.html
