The editor requested project-specific method wording. A further generic PRNG remedy appeared on projects with no random step, including #05, #08, #13, #23, #39, #57, #58 and #70; the coordinator removed it from unrelated chapter and PLAN entries. 3. **Missing-capability scope (corrected by coordinator during review):** The original fixed-data workaround sentence implied that preset values could replace live clock/audio/speech behavior. Chapters and PLANs #14, #19, #62, #67, #71 and #74 now state project-specific limits. #74 explicitly says text or fixed data do not create speech. 5. **#36 Hourglass output (corrected):** The source starts with three grains in rows 0, 1 and 2, while the initial chapter's `start` block showed an empty first row. The first row now shows `| o |`, matching source, prose and subsequent ticks.
6. **Placeholder opening sentence (corrected and searched):** All 52 project-data paragraphs originally ended with `เพื่อให้ผู้อ่านเห็นทั้งสถานะก่อนและหลังแต่ละคำสั่ง`.
