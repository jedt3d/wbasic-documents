# Project 58 Plan: Animate a Text Rainbow

Status: Awaiting implementation and verification; no main.wbas exists yet.

Goal: Animate a Text Rainbow.
Data: seven colors and the pattern position.
Method: Shift colors each tick, with a monochrome fallback.
Acceptance check: After seven ticks, the pattern returns to its starting position.
Remaining gap: Verify terminal color capability and the TUI timer.

Credit: Al Sweigart, author of The Big Book of Small Python Projects. This WBasic chapter plan reworks the idea in new words.
